The research

From awareness to circular action

The study brings together Sustainable Consumption, Circular Economy principles and Behavioural Economics to investigate the attitude–behaviour gap among Bengaluru youth.

01 · Challenge

Awareness is not enough

Unsustainable consumption contributes to resource depletion, climate pressures and waste. Youth awareness can be high while everyday choices remain inconsistent.

02 · Investigation

Measure the determinants

The study examines clothing, electronic gadgets and everyday lifestyle choices, alongside economic, social and behavioural influences.

03 · Model

Design the bridge

Findings inform a Behavioural Circular Economy Model showing how targeted interventions may help move people from awareness and intention toward responsible action.

SDG 12 connection: The study is aligned with Responsible Consumption and Production by examining behavioural conditions that can support more sustainable consumption.
